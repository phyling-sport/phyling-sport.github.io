Mini-Phyling GPS

Mini-Phyling GPS

The Mini-Phyling GPS combines a 9-axis inertial measurement unit with a multi-constellation GNSS receiver in a waterproof IPX8 housing weighing only 25 g. It works like the Mini-Phyling V2 and adds GPS position and speed measurement.

Overview

The Mini-Phyling GPS records locally or streams its data in real time over Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE) to the Phyling application via a Maxi-Phyling. Motion and positioning are measured simultaneously and synchronized in a single recording.

Features and Use Cases

The Mini-Phyling GPS was designed to capture motion and GPS position in activities where space is very limited. Small, self-contained and waterproof (IPX8), it measures both movement and GPS position where a standard unit cannot be installed.

  • Open-water swimming: Slipped into a swim cap to track trajectory, speed and stroke rate.
  • Kitesurfing, windsurfing and light sailing: Attached to the wing or rigging to analyze sail movements and speed on the water.
  • Surfing, stand-up paddle and kayaking: Positioned on the board or boat to relate speed, heading and craft movements.
  • Skiing and board sports: Integrated into the helmet or back protector to measure speed, trajectory and impacts.
  • Trail running and orienteering: Worn close to the body to associate stride and course.
  • Sports industry: Field validation of equipment prototypes (boards, wings, boats, helmets) in real conditions, without modifying the equipment.
