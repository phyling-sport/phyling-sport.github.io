Tracking app

Tracking app

The Phyling tracking app lets you follow your athletes live, built for training. Follow your athletes on the map during the session, replay it with them afterwards and share it in a few seconds.

▶  See an exampleOpen the tracking app  ↗

Key benefits

Compatible with your Phyling sensors

Track your athletes with Maxi-Phyling and Phyling-LTE devices, with no special setup.

Very low-latency live and replay

Positions and data appear on the map with minimal delay. Once the session is over, replay it at your own pace from the playback bar.

Sharing by link or QR code

Share the session with a simple link, or a QR code: family, staff and partners can follow your athletes. You choose which data they can see.

Simple training or organized sessions

Start a training session in a few clicks. You can also organize a session in race mode, with a GPX route, laps and a start.

Teams, groups and withdrawals

Organize your athletes into teams or groups, and flag an athlete's withdrawal during a session.

Real-time ranking

Follow your athletes' live ranking, with groups and the gaps between them: a simple tool to steer the session.

Races and TV broadcast

For race broadcasting (public tracking, TV integration, video overlay of the data from our sensors), we work with GeoRacing, a race tracking specialist. Discover GeoRacing
